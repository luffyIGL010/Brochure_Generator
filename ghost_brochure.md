# Ghost

## Company Overview

Ghost is a powerful, open-source application designed for professional publishers, creators, and businesses to create, share, and grow a business around their content. It serves as an all-in-one platform for building websites, publishing content, sending newsletters, and managing paid subscriptions.

Ghost distinguishes itself by offering complete control over branding and design while providing modern tools for audience growth and revenue generation. The platform is independent, open-source, and funded 100% by its users, with no external investors.

## Mission & Vision

**Mission**
To empower professional publishers and creators to escape algorithmic dependencies and own their audience, brand, and revenue entirely. Ghost aims to make it easy to build independent media businesses and content marketing operations that are measurable and sustainable.

**Core Values & Principles**
*   **Independence:** Funded entirely by users, ensuring the product remains aligned with creator needs rather than investor pressures.
*   **Open Source:** Committed to transparency and community-driven development.
*   **Creator-Centric:** Designed to be "calm by design" with advanced workflows, allowing creators to immerse themselves in storytelling without technical friction.
*   **Reliability:** Built to last, with a focus on stability and long-term support for businesses.

## Products & Services

Ghost provides a comprehensive suite of tools for content creation, distribution, and monetization:

*   **Website Builder & Design**
    *   **Custom Branding:** Complete control over website design and branding to match specific styles.
    *   **Theme Marketplace:** Access to hundreds of custom themes or the ability to build custom designs from scratch.
    *   **Easy Site Design:** Intuitive settings to launch and tweak websites without complex coding.

*   **Advanced Creator Tools**
    *   **Professional Editor:** A ground-up built editor with invisible interfaces until needed, supporting rich media including image galleries, GIFs, video, audio, products, info boxes, and downloadable files.
    *   **Built-in Newsletters:** Native email delivery for posts, allowing for segmented newsletters based on audience preferences.

*   **Audience Growth & Monetization**
    *   **Membership Management:** Native signup forms to convert anonymous visitors into logged-in members.
    *   **Paid Subscriptions:** Support for free, monthly, and yearly premium tiers.
    *   **Offers & Promotions:** Tools to create discounts, sales events, and special promotions to drive subscriber growth.

*   **Business Management & Analytics**
    *   **Native Analytics:** Detailed engagement metrics to understand content performance and identify core audience segments.
    *   **Member Insights:** Visibility into signups, payments, and reading habits.
    *   **Integrations Library:** Extensive compatibility with social media, payment gateways (e.g., Stripe), email marketing, analytics, and other third-party services.

## Industries & Customers

Ghost serves a diverse range of content-driven businesses and individuals:

*   **Publishers & Media Outlets:** Independent media businesses looking to build sustainable revenue models (e.g., 404 Media, Platformer, The Lever).
*   **Creators & Influencers:** Individuals in travel, science, design, productivity, and other niches seeking to own their audience and revenue (e.g., Gone With The Wynns, Jeff Su).
*   **Businesses & Brands:** Organizations using Ghost for content marketing, lead generation, and brand storytelling (e.g., YCombinator, Kickstarter, Buffer, Unsplash).

**Key Business Problems Addressed:**
*   Escaping algorithmic control of social media platforms.
*   Simplifying the complexity of building membership and subscription businesses.
*   Providing measurable content marketing through native lead generation and analytics.
